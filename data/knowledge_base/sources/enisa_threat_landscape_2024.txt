Source summary: ENISA Threat Landscape 2024

This public ENISA report describes major cybersecurity threats, threat trends, attack techniques, and mitigation considerations using publicly reported incidents. For this project, it supports a defensive research direction: organize threat hypotheses by attack technique, affected process, observable signals, mitigation, and uncertainty. It should inform broad taxonomy and evaluation questions, not reproduce incidents or provide instructions for attacking systems.

Relevant project use: threat taxonomy, mitigation framing, public evidence grounding.
Safety boundary: summary only; no personal data, credentials, production transactions, live targets, or operational exploit steps.
